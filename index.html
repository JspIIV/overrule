Give your agent a wallet. Let Internet Court take it back.

You give an AI agent a MetaMask delegation to spend from your smart account, under a mandate you write in plain words and sign. Every payment it makes is recorded. Anybody can ask Internet Court to review them: GenLayer validators judge the payments against your mandate, and if the agent broke it, the court revokes the delegation itself, the moment its verdict is final. No relayer, no key, nobody in between.

Internet Court · GenLayer · MetaMask Delegation Framework v1.3.0 (ERC-7710)

1. A signed mandateMetaMask's DelegationManager and HybridDeleGator smart account, unmodified, on GenLayer Chain. One caveat carries the keccak256 of your mandate, so it is part of what you signed.
2. Every payment on the recordThe CourtEnforcer caveat records each payment and the agent's own memo. Only MetaMask's DelegationManager can write to it.
3. The court enforcesA GenLayer round judges the payments. OVERRULE is final when consensus is, and the court calls the enforcer itself: the next redemption reverts.

Try it with one wallet

You play both sides: the person who delegates and the agent who spends. Each step is one MetaMask action on GenLayer Chain. The test dollars are worth nothing.
