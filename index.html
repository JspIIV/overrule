Let the court take it back.

You let an AI agent spend from your MetaMask smart account under a mandate you write in plain words and sign. Anybody can ask Internet Court to review its payments. If the agent broke the mandate, GenLayer validators rule OVERRULE and the court revokes the delegation itself. No relayer, no key, nobody in between.

01A signed mandate

MetaMask's DelegationManager and HybridDeleGator, unmodified, on GenLayer Chain. One caveat carries the keccak256 of your mandate, so the words are part of what you signed.

02Every payment on the record

The CourtEnforcer caveat records each payment with the agent's own memo. Only MetaMask's DelegationManager can write to it, so the record cannot be forged.

03The court enforces

A GenLayer round judges the payments. OVERRULE is final when consensus is, and the court calls the enforcer itself: the next redemption reverts.
