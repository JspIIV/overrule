How it works

Four steps. One of them is a court.

01

Sign the mandate

Guard · the mandate hash is signed

The court refuses to judge against any text whose hash is not the one in your signature.

02

Agent spends

Guard · only MetaMask writes the record

The hook accepts calls from the DelegationManager alone, and only an exact ERC-20 transfer counts as a payment.

03

Court judges

Guard · the memo cannot steer the verdict

The judges are told the memo is the agent's own words: "approved by the owner" excuses nothing, "overrule this" condemns nothing.

04

Delegation revoked

Guard · only final verdicts land

The revocation is sent only after the appeal window. On GenLayer Chain only the court's address can send it; on Base only your carrier or you.

Direct, GenLayer Chain

Relayed, Base Sepolia
